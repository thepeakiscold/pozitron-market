Privacy Policy for FocusedLife

Effective Date: October 4, 2026 • Version 1.0

FocusedLife does not collect, transmit, share, or monetize any personal data. All study logs, topics, and settings remain solely and securely on your own device.

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

FocusedLife is a distraction-free, privacy-first study timer and habit analytics tool. We believe that your focus habits, notes, and academic schedules are personal. Therefore, our architecture is strictly local-first and offline-capable.

2. No Account Required

You can use all features of FocusedLife without providing an email, username, phone number, or password. There is no remote account registration.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The application only accesses and saves information in your device's private local application storage:

  • Study Sessions: Start timestamp, duration, and optional focus notes entered by you.
  • Topics & Targets: Custom topic titles, chosen color accents, emoji icons, and weekly target hours.
  • Settings: Timer preferences, volume settings for ambient soundscapes, language selection, and UI theme.

4. No Remote Servers or Tracking

FocusedLife does not operate remote servers to harvest analytics, nor does it embed third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or profiling SDKs.

5. Data Deletion and Control

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • You can reset or erase all study sessions and topics inside the app's Settings menu.
  • Uninstalling the FocusedLife app completely and irreversibly removes all stored records from your device.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy statement, please contact the developer at: support@focusedlife.app.
